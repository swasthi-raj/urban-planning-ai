311 Complaints vs. AI-Detected Damage AI 311 Key Finding
Compares AI-detected damage against 311 complaint volume, using the full annotated dataset. A high AI-to-311 ratio signals underreporting — real damage present but not formally reported by residents. Lower-income neighborhoods show markedly higher underreporting ratios — a stronger and more policy-actionable equity signal than raw damage rate alone.

📌 Reading this table: Barrio Logan (lowest income) shows AI detecting roughly 3x more damage than residents formally reported — versus Mission Hills and La Jolla (highest income), where reported complaints roughly match or exceed AI-detected damage. This suggests a complaint-driven repair budget would systematically underserve lower-income neighborhoods, regardless of actual damage severity patterns.
Detection Reliability by Type
Model accuracy varies by defect type. Sidewalk, cracks, and bike lane detection are strong; pothole detection is the weakest category and should be treated as lower-confidence until further training improves it.
